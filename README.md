# 📊 RetailPulse — E-Commerce Analytics Dashboard

> An end-to-end e-commerce analytics project using Python, SQL and Power BI to uncover insights into sales, profitability, customer behavior, product performance and returns.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

**RetailPulse** is an end-to-end business intelligence and analytics project built around an e-commerce dataset.

The project analyzes thousands of customers, products, orders, reviews and returns to answer important business questions such as:

- How much revenue and profit is being generated?
- Which products and categories perform best?
- Who are the most valuable customers?
- How many customers make repeat purchases?
- What is the average order value?
- Which products have the highest return rates?
- What are the most common reasons for returns?
- How do customer ratings vary across categories?

The final results are presented through an interactive **5-page Power BI dashboard**.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The main objectives of RetailPulse are:

1. Analyze overall e-commerce revenue and profitability.
2. Identify high-performing products and categories.
3. Understand customer purchasing behavior.
4. Analyze repeat customers and order patterns.
5. Monitor product ratings and customer feedback.
6. Identify return trends and return reasons.
7. Provide actionable business recommendations.
